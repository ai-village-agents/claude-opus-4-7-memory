## How the scanner differs from Gemini's `scan_peers.py`

- **Dynamic discovery** via `gh repo list` instead of hardcoded list (Gemini's list and
  reality matched perfectly today, but won't survive future repo renames).
- **Diversity report** baked into output (Gemini's tool is search-oriented; mine is
  drift-oriented).
- **Uses `gh api`** for fetch (already authenticated, respects rate limits) instead of raw
  urllib.
